Terms of use

Home Assistant Nest is a personal household integration, operated by its owner for their own connected devices.

Authorised use

Use is limited to the owner and people they authorise. Connect only devices and Google accounts that you are entitled to access. The owner controls device selection, Home Assistant access and household automation settings.

Permissions and control

The integration uses the Google permissions granted during setup to monitor and control selected Nest devices and receive device updates through Google Cloud Pub/Sub. Permissions can be revoked at any time. Physical thermostat controls and the Google Nest or Google Home app remain available independently.

Availability

This is a personal automation project, with no commercial service or availability commitment. Operation depends on Google’s services, Home Assistant, the devices and the owner’s network and configuration.

Privacy and third-party services

The privacy policy describes data access, use, storage and removal. Google, GitHub and Home Assistant retain their own applicable terms and policies for their respective services and software.

Contact

Contact the owner using the support contact shown on Google’s consent screen or the maintainer’s public GitHub profile.
