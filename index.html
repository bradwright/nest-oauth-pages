Personal household integration

Heating, connected.

Home Assistant Nest connects selected Google Nest devices to the owner’s private Home Assistant installation.

What the integration does

It reads thermostat temperature, humidity, heating activity and settings so the owner can monitor heating and coordinate household automations. With permission, Home Assistant can also change thermostat modes and temperature setpoints.

The Google authorisation flow lets the owner choose which Nest devices to connect. Google Cloud Pub/Sub delivers device updates to Home Assistant.

Personal use, controlled access

This is a personal integration for the owner and their authorised household. Access to connected devices depends on the permissions granted through Google and the owner’s Home Assistant access controls.

This public website explains the integration. It does not offer a thermostat dashboard, collect Google credentials or receive thermostat readings.

Read the privacy policy for data use, storage and revocation details, and the terms of use for the scope of this personal integration.
